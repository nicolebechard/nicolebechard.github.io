FrigoTime Privacy Notice (Beta)

Last updated: September 29, 2026

FrigoTime is a small food waste app in beta testing. This page explains what personal data I collect from beta testers and applicants, why, who else handles it, and what your rights are. I have tried to keep it short and plain.

1. Who is responsible for your data

I am. I am an individual based in Prague, Czech Republic, and I run FrigoTime.
Contact for anything privacy related, including deletion requests: frigotimeapp@gmail.com

Receipt images are not stored by FrigoTime. A photo is used only to extract the items and is not kept in my database. Receipts can contain personal details such as a name, a loyalty card number, or part of a card number. Please crop or avoid including these. The image is sent to Anthropic for reading (see section 3), and Anthropic deletes it within 30 days under its own terms.

Incidental technical logging. When a receipt scan fails, a short fragment of the extracted text can appear in my hosting provider's error logs while I debug the failure. This never happens on a successful scan. If sending an alert email fails, the attempt is logged with your email address and the number of items in that alert. These are automatic error records, not something I read routinely, and they follow the retention periods in section 5.

I do not make decisions about you by automated means. I approve or decline applications myself, by hand.

The app uses only what it needs to keep you logged in. No advertising or analytics tools.

4. Transfers outside the EU

Anthropic and Resend process data in the United States, Vercel's account and control-plane data is handled in the United States, and Supabase's contracting entity is in Singapore. Anthropic, Resend, and Supabase have signed data processing terms with me covering these transfers, using the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Resend is also certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Vercel hosts the app under its standard terms; Vercel's formal data processing addendum is available only on paid plans, which this beta does not currently use. Tally keeps data in the EU.

5. How long I keep it

  • Declined or unanswered applications: deleted 30 days after I answer.
  • Accounts and food data: kept while you are actively using the app. If you leave the beta, or if your account has no login for 90 days, I treat that as you having stopped, and delete your account and food data 90 days after that point.
  • Scan and login records: kept on the same schedule as your account. Once deleted, I may keep an anonymized, aggregate summary that cannot be traced back to you, for example a general pattern across all testers.
  • Feedback: kept in Tally for 90 days.
  • Hosting error logs (Vercel): kept for roughly 1 hour, then gone.
  • Email provider logs: Resend keeps email logs for up to 30 days.
  • Anthropic: deletes receipt scan inputs and outputs within 30 days, unless flagged for misuse, in which case it can be kept up to 2 years.

6. Your rights

You can ask me at any time to: see the data I hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict how I use it, export it, or object to a use based on legitimate interest. If you gave consent, you can withdraw it at any time, which does not affect what happened before. Email frigotimeapp@gmail.com. I will answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (uoou.gov.cz), or to the authority in the country where you live.

7. Security

Your data is stored in access-controlled databases, and logins use a one-time code sent by email. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, I will tell you.

8. Changes

If I change how I handle data, I will update this page and change the date at the top. For anything significant I will email active testers.
